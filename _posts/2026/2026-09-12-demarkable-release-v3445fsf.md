##  About

Every domain sending email gets DMARC aggregate reports and SMTP TLS reports back from Google, Microsoft, Yahoo, and every other major mailbox provider — dense XML and JSON files nobody actually enjoys reading. Demarkable turns them into something you can actually understand at a glance with a drag and drop and a simpe viewer.
