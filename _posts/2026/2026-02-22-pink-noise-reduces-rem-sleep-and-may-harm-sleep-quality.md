> Pink noise--often used to promote sleep--may reduce restorative REM sleep and interfere with sleep recovery. In contrast, earplugs were found to be significantly more effective in protecting sleep against traffic noise, [according to new study published in the journal](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/sleep/zsag001)_[Sleep](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/sleep/zsag001) \_from the University of Pennsylvania Perelman School of Medicine_.
