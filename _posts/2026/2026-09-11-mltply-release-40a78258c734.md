## About

Mltply is the chat maths buddy for kids, in a chat-like interface kids can be quizzed on maths questions in increasing complexity - fully customisable in the settings to target particularly weak areas or simply at the age appropriate level e.g. simple addition 1-10 for beginners up to square root of double digits for more advanced.
