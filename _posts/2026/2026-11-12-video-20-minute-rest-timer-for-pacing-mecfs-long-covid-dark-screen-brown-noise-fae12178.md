A 20 minute rest timer made for pacing, by someone with ME/CFS.

Press play, put the screen down and rest. Nothing flashes, nothing talks, and nothing changes suddenly.

What happens
-----------------------

- A near-black screen with dim grey text, so it is easy on light-sensitive eyes.
- Steady brown noise at a low volume. Turn it up to suit you, or mute it.
- The screen changes once a minute to show how much rest is left. There is no ticking clock.
- The noise fades out at the end, then three soft chimes tell you the rest is over. Take your time getting up.

Brown noise is a deep, low rumble, like distant surf or a fan in another room.

Why I made it
---------------------

I have ME/CFS and I rest on a timer through the day. Phone alarms are harsh and most timer videos are bright, busy or have music. I wanted one I could tolerate on a bad day.

Other lengths and sounds are in the Pacing Rest playlist on this channel.
