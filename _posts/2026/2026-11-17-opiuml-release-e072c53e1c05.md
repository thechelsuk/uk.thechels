## About

Opiuml is a podcast player built around two lists: an Inbox for everything new, and a Queue for what you actually want to hear.

More about [Opiuml](https://opiuml.thechels.uk).

## What's Changed

- Find new podcasts, and see a show before you subscribe
- Find podcasts now shows Apple's top charts for your country, with categories such as Comedy, History and True Crime.
- Tap a show in search or the charts to see its description, recent episodes and when the last one came out, then subscribe from there.
- Storage settings now explain that downloads are deleted once an episode is played or marked as played, unless you turn that off for a podcast.
