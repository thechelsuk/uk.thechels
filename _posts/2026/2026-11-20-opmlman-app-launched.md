Today I'm announcing the release of OPMLMan for iOS. As it sounds the app helps manage and check OPML files (RSS, YouTube, Podcasts).

Open the export OPML file from your feed reader or podcast app, tap Check, and it tells you which feeds are active, which have gone quiet, which have moved and which are gone for good.

Each feed gets a status: active, slowing, dormant, moved, not found or failing. You also see its latest item, how often it publishes and what the server said. Slowing means nothing new for 30 days and dormant means nothing for 90, and you can change both in Settings. A moved feed is one tap to follow. Nothing is checked until you ask, so nothing runs in the background.

It finds duplicates too: the same feed written two ways, over http and https, or one address redirecting to another. You choose what goes. Then you can merge lists, split one into podcasts, video and articles, and export clean OPML for the apps that read each.

Every file you import stays its own collection, with its folders. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac (as a native app) and Apple Vision Pro, kept in step through your own iCloud.

It's free, with no ads, no tracking and no account. There's a tip jar if you'd like to say thanks;

OPMLMan doesn't play podcasts or show articles: for podcasts, export a clean list and bring it into [Opiuml](https://opiuml.thechels.uk), my podcast player.

[OPMLMan is on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/opmlman-feeds-manager/id6832117624), and there's more at [opmlman.thechels.uk](https://opmlman.thechels.uk).
