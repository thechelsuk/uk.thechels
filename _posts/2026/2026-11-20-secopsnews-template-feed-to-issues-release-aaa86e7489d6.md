## About

A GitHub template repository that watches the RSS and Atom feeds your team cares about, such as vendor advisories, status pages and security blogs, and opens each new item as a GitHub issue. It's the set-up behind [SecOpsNews](https://secops.thechels.uk), cut down to one list of feeds to edit.
