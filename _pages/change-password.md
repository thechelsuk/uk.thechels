Yeh, no. This is a problem. This website doesn't have any form of membership, login, authentication, nor backend.

Take a break and put the kettle on, but seriously, no you cannot change a password you have never set.

So why does this page exist? Because it is good practice as set out by the [W3C](https://w3c.github.io/webappsec-change-password-url/) as part of the [.well-known URI standards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Well-known_URI).
