OPMLMan opens the OPML file from your feed reader or podcast app, checks every feed, and tells you which are active, which have gone quiet, which have moved and which are gone for good. Then it helps you tidy up and export a clean list.

View [marketing site](https://opmlman.thechels.uk) &rarr;

## Highlights

- Feed health: every feed shows as active, slowing, dormant, moved, not found or failing.
- Nothing is checked until you tap Check, so nothing runs in the background and no feed gets hammered.
- See each feed's latest item, how often it publishes, and what the server said.
- Duplicates: the same feed written two ways, http and https, or one address redirecting to another. You choose what goes.
- Follow moved addresses with one tap.
- Collections: every file you import stays its own collection, with its folders. Merge lists into a new one, or split one into podcasts, video and articles.
- Clean OPML export per collection, combined, or one file per type.
- iPhone, iPad and a native Mac app, in step through your own iCloud.
- Private by design: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. It only talks to the feeds you check.
- [App Privacy Policy](/app/privacy).

## FAQ

### How much does OPMLMan cost?

Nothing. It's free, with no ads and no tracking. There's an optional tip jar if you'd like to say thanks; it unlocks nothing.

### What is an OPML file?

The list format feed readers and podcast apps use to export and import subscriptions. Look for Export OPML or Export subscriptions in your app's settings.

### Does it play podcasts or show articles?

No. OPMLMan manages and checks your lists. For podcasts, export a clean list and import it into [Opiuml](/projects/opiuml), from the same developer.

### Which devices does it support?

iPhone, iPad and Mac.

### Where can I get it?

OPMLMan is [on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6832117624) for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
