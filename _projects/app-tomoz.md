Tomoz makes it easy to plan for tomorrow today. Pick and choose calendars, add in reminders and get a clear unified view of tomorrow.

View [marketing site](https://tomoz.thechels.uk) &rarr;

## Highlights

- Built in SwiftUI experience that surfaces only tomorrow's events in a clean, simple interface.
- Multiple calendar support and reminder integration.
- Export to Markdown, CSV, or JSON for sharing via iCloud Drive, email, or automations.
- Personalise the interface with custom colour themes, tint controls, and Light/Dark/System modes.
- Offline-first design keeps calendar data on-device with no analytics or cloud syncing.
- Ideal for busy professionals, students, or teams who want to share a focused plan.
