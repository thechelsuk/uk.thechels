Opiuml is a podcast player built around two lists: an Inbox for everything new, and a Queue for what you actually want to hear. New episodes arrive in the Inbox, or go straight into your Queue to play next or last, and the rest quietly tidies itself away.

View [marketing site](https://opiuml.thechels.uk) &rarr;

## Highlights

- Inbox and Queue: triage new episodes with a swipe to queue next, queue last or archive.
- Per-podcast rules for where new episodes go, how many to keep, playback speed and skip intervals.
- Keep only the latest, so daily news shows hold just today's episode and old ones disappear.
- Chapters, artwork and show notes, with chapter skipping on the lock screen.
- Video podcasts, YouTube channels via RSS, and your own audio files.
- Send episodes to Apple Watch, and listen in CarPlay.
- Groups, OPML import and export, and a list of podcasts that have gone quiet.
- Listening history and stats: time listened, time saved and top shows.
- Siri Shortcuts and deep links for automation.
- Private by design: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your library lives on your device and syncs through your own iCloud.
- [App Privacy Policy](/app/privacy).

## FAQ

### How much does Opiuml cost?

Opiuml is free to download. Unlocking it is £29.99 a year, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers.

### Can I bring my podcasts from another app?

Yes. Export an OPML file from your current podcast player and import it into Opiuml.

### Which devices does it support?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later, and CarPlay.

### Where can I get it?

Opiuml is [available now on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/opiuml-podcast-player/id6823693348) for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay.
